ABOUT SMASHX

SmashX MMA and Fitness is more than just a gym in Mulund—it's a proving ground. We bring together individuals of all levels, from beginners taking their first steps to seasoned athletes.

🔥

WHAT WE BELIEVE

We believe in discipline over motivation. Motivation fades, but the habits forged on these mats last a lifetime. Leave your ego at the door and come ready to work.

🥊

WHY MMA?

Mixed Martial Arts is the ultimate test of physical movement and mental strategy. It combines the power of Boxing and Muay Thai with the chess-like ground control of BJJ.

🤝

WHO IT'S FOR

Whether you're a day-one beginner looking to get into the best shape of your life, or an aspiring competitor chasing gold, SmashX is your tribe.
